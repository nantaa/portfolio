How It's Made

Procedural Archipelago & Water Volume

The 400×400 heightfield carves undercut Wayag-style mushroom karst islets with a wave-cut notch, baked horizon ambient occlusion, and 1,500 instanced broadleaf, palm, and pandanus canopy clusters.

Water is rendered via a two-pass depth-peeled volume with Beer–Lambert absorption, screen-space refraction, 26-step raymarched reflections, volumetric God-rays, and a 256×256 HalfFloat ping-pong GPU wave equation with land-mask reflection.

Choreography Integrity: Verified (0 collisions)
