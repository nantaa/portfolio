TECHNICAL NOTES

Ulun Danu, Lake Bratan

This miniature slice of Bedugul's water temple is synthesized in a single self-contained HTML file using Three.js r160 with zero external models, textures, or audio assets.

  • Volumetric Water Block: Rendered via an MSAA HalfFloat offscreen pass with depth buffer reconstruction, Beer–Lambert absorption, 26-step screen-space reflections (SSR), and screen-space refraction inside a rounded 10×10 SDF volume.
  • 60 Hz GPU Wave Equation: A 256×256 ping-pong simulation reflects circular and capsule wakes off an island land-mask texture, driven by cursor motion, duck wakes, raindrops, and temple bell resonances.
  • Merged Architecture & Botanicals: The 11-tier meru, split gate (candi bentar), bale pavilion, and swaying bamboo penjor are assembled procedurally and merged into one vertex-colored mesh and one emissive night-glow mesh.
  • CPU-Baked Horizon AO: The 400×400 heightfield computes per-vertex horizon ambient occlusion, canopy darkening, underwater algae, and animated caustics.
